# 5. What are Missing Values?

One of the most asked viva topics.

## Definition

Missing values are empty or unavailable values in a dataset.

Example:

| Marks |
| ----- |
| 85    |
| NaN   |
| 90    |

NaN = Not a Number

# 8. Methods to Handle Missing Values

Very important.

---

## A) Deletion Method

Rows with missing values are removed.

### Advantage

Simple method.

### Disadvantage

Loss of important data.

---

## B) Imputation Method

Missing values are replaced with estimated values.

This practical mainly uses imputation. 

---

# 9. What is Imputation?

## Definition

Imputation is the process of filling missing values using statistical methods.

---

# 10. Types of Imputation

---

## Mean Imputation

Missing value replaced using average.

Formula:

\bar{x} = \frac{\sum x}{n}

### Used For

Normally distributed numerical data.

---

## Median Imputation

Missing value replaced using middle value.

### Best For

Skewed data and outliers.

---

## Mode Imputation

Missing value replaced using most frequent value.

### Used For

Categorical data.

---

# 13. What are Outliers?

Most important viva topic in Practical 2.

## Definition

Outliers are extreme values that are very different from other data points.

Example:

```text id="lfk7j0"
Marks:
70, 75, 72, 74, 73, 250
```

250 is an outlier.

---

* Measurement errors
* Rare events
* Experimental errors
* Natural variation

---

# 15. Why Outliers are Problematic?

## Viva Answer

Outliers can:

* distort statistical calculations
* affect mean
* reduce model accuracy
* create misleading analysis

---

# 16. What is IQR? (Very Important)

## Definition

IQR stands for Interquartile Range.

It measures the spread of middle 50% of data.

The manual uses IQR for outlier detection. 

---

# 17. Quartiles

## Q1 (First Quartile)

25th percentile.

## Q2

Median (50th percentile).

## Q3

75th percentile.

---

# 18. IQR Formula

IQR = Q_3 - Q_1

---

# 19. Outlier Detection Formula

Values outside these limits are considered outliers:

Lower\ Bound = Q_1 - 1.5 \times IQR

Upper\ Bound = Q_3 + 1.5 \times IQR

---

# 20. Why IQR is Preferred?

## Viva Answer

IQR is preferred because:

* robust to outliers
* works well for skewed data
* simple and effective

---

Clipping restricts values within lower and upper boundaries.

# 25. What is Skewness?

## Definition

Skewness measures asymmetry in data distribution.

The manual checks skewness before and after transformation. 

---

# 26. Types of Skewness

---

## Positive Skew

Tail on right side.

```text id="1u61qm"
Most values small, few very large
```

Mean > Median

---

## Negative Skew

Tail on left side.

Mean < Median

---

## Symmetrical Distribution

Mean ≈ Median

---

# 29. Log Transformation Formula
