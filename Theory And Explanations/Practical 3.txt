# 1. What is Descriptive Statistics?

## Definition

Descriptive statistics is the branch of statistics used to summarize, organize, and describe the important characteristics of a dataset. 

## Simple Meaning

Instead of observing thousands of data values individually, descriptive statistics gives a compact summary using numbers and graphs.

---

# 2. Types of Descriptive Statistics

According to the manual, there are two main types: 

1. Measures of Central Tendency
2. Measures of Variability (Spread)

---

# 3. Measures of Central Tendency

## Definition

These measures describe the center or typical value of a dataset.

Main measures:

* Mean
* Median
* Mode

---

# 4. Mean

Most important concept.

## Definition

Mean is the average of all observations.

Formula:

\bar{x} = \frac{\sum x}{n}

Where:

* Σx = sum of all values
* n = total number of observations

---

## Example

```text id="h2v4xh"
Marks = 70, 80, 90
Mean = (70+80+90)/3 = 80
```

---

## Advantages of Mean

* Easy to calculate
* Uses all observations
* Widely used in ML/statistics

---

## Disadvantages of Mean

* Affected by outliers
* Not suitable for skewed data

---

# 5. Median

## Definition

Median is the middle value after arranging data in ascending order.

---

## Example

```text id="6j3y0w"
2, 4, 6, 8, 10
Median = 6
```

---

## Important Property

Median is less affected by outliers.

---

# 6. Mean vs Median

| Mean                         | Median                |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Arithmetic average           | Middle value          |
| Sensitive to outliers        | Resistant to outliers |
| Best for normal distribution | Best for skewed data  |

---

# 7. Mode

## Definition

Mode is the most frequently occurring value in dataset.

---

## Example

```text id="9z9v65"
2, 3, 3, 4, 5
Mode = 3
```

---

# 8. Measures of Variability (Spread)

## Definition

These measures show how much data values are spread out from the center.

Main measures:

* Range
* Variance
* Standard Deviation
* Quartiles
* Percentiles

---

# 9. Range

## Definition

Difference between maximum and minimum value.

Formula:

Range = Maximum - Minimum

---

## Example

```text id="16pv9g"
Max = 100
Min = 40
Range = 60
```

---

# 10. Variance

Very important viva topic.

## Definition

Variance measures how far data points are spread from the mean.

Small variance:
→ data points close to mean

Large variance:
→ data points widely spread

---

## Formula

\sigma^2 = \frac{\sum (x - \mu)^2}{N}

---

# 11. Standard Deviation

Most asked viva question.

## Definition

Standard deviation measures average deviation from mean.

It is the square root of variance.

---

## Formula

\sigma = \sqrt{\frac{\sum (x - \mu)^2}{N}}

---

## Interpretation

| Standard Deviation | Meaning            |
| ------------------ | ------------------ |
| Low                | Data close to mean |
| High               | Data highly spread |

---

# 12. Why Standard Deviation is Important?

## Viva Answer

It helps understand consistency and variability in data.

Used heavily in:

* ML
* finance
* analytics
* quality control

---

# 13. Difference Between Variance and Standard Deviation

| Variance             | Standard Deviation      |
| -------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Square of deviations | Square root of variance |
| Unit squared         | Original unit           |
| Harder to interpret  | Easier to interpret     |

---

# 14. Percentiles

Important topic.

## Definition

Percentiles divide data into 100 equal parts.

Example:

* 25th percentile → 25% data below it
* 90th percentile → 90% data below it

Manual calculates percentiles using NumPy. 

---

# 15. Quartiles

## Definition

Quartiles divide data into four equal parts.

| Quartile | Meaning         |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Q1       | 25th percentile |

| Q3       | 75th percentile |

# 19. What is Distribution?

## Definition

Distribution describes how data values are spread across dataset.

---

# 20. Types of Distribution

---

## Symmetric Distribution

Equal spread on both sides.

Mean ≈ Median

---

## Positively Skewed Distribution

Right tail longer.

Mean > Median

---

## Negatively Skewed Distribution

Left tail longer.

Mean < Median
