# Practical 7: Text Analytics — Document Preprocessing and TF-IDF

## What We Are Doing in This Practical

In this practical, we perform **text preprocessing** on a sample document using NLP (Natural Language Processing) techniques such as:

* Tokenization
* POS Tagging
* Stop Words Removal
* Stemming
* Lemmatization

After preprocessing, we calculate:

* Term Frequency (TF)
* Inverse Document Frequency (IDF)
* TF-IDF representation of text 

This practical is important because it introduces:

* Natural Language Processing (NLP)
* Text cleaning
* Text representation for ML models

---

# 1. What is Text Analytics?

## Definition

Text analytics is the process of extracting meaningful information from unstructured text data using NLP techniques. 

---

# 2. What is NLP (Natural Language Processing)?

## Definition

NLP is a branch of Artificial Intelligence that enables computers to understand, process, and analyze human language.

---

# 3. What is Unstructured Data?

## Definition

Data without fixed format or table structure.

Examples:

* Emails
* Social media posts
* Reviews
* Articles
* Chats

---

# 4. Why Text Preprocessing is Needed?

Most important viva topic.

## Viva Answer

Raw text contains unnecessary words, symbols, and inconsistencies. Preprocessing cleans and converts text into useful format for machine learning and analysis.

---

# 5. What is Tokenization?

Most asked question.

## Definition

Tokenization is the process of splitting text into smaller units called tokens. 

---

## Example

```text id="vpkjcm"
Input:
"Text analytics is fun"

Output:
["Text", "analytics", "is", "fun"]
```

---

# 6. What are Tokens?

## Definition

Tokens are individual words, symbols, or subwords extracted from text.

---

# 7. What is POS Tagging?

Important topic.

## Definition

POS (Part-of-Speech) tagging identifies grammatical category of each word. 

---

## Examples of POS Tags

| Tag | Meaning   |
| --- | --------- |
| NN  | Noun      |
| VB  | Verb      |
| JJ  | Adjective |

---

# 8. Why POS Tagging is Used?

## Viva Answer

To understand grammatical structure and meaning of sentence.

---

# 9. What are Stop Words?

## Definition

Stop words are common words that carry little useful meaning in analysis.

Examples:

```text id="u7h1q8"
is, the, a, an, in
```

---

# 10. Stop Words Removal

## Definition

Removing unnecessary common words from text.

---

# 11. Why Remove Stop Words?

## Viva Answer

Because they increase noise and do not contribute much meaning to analysis.

---

# 12. What is Stemming?

Very important viva topic.

## Definition

Stemming reduces words to root form, which may not always be a valid dictionary word. 

---

## Example

```text id="c6ol3z"
running → run
playing → play
```

---

# 13. What is Lemmatization?

## Definition

Lemmatization reduces words to meaningful root words using vocabulary and grammar rules. 

---

## Example

```text id="sgcr6f"
better → good
running → run
```

---

# 14. Difference Between Stemming and Lemmatization

| Stemming                  | Lemmatization               |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Faster                    | More accurate               |
| May produce invalid words | Produces meaningful words   |
| Rule-based cutting        | Uses vocabulary and grammar |

---

# 15. What is Corpus?

## Definition

A corpus is a collection of documents or text data.

---

# 16. What is Vectorization?

## Definition

Converting text into numerical representation for machine learning.

---

# 17. What is Term Frequency (TF)?

Most important viva topic.

## Definition

Term Frequency measures how frequently a word appears in a document. 

Formula:

TF(t)=\frac{\text{Count of term }t}{\text{Total terms in document}}

---

# 18. What is Inverse Document Frequency (IDF)?

## Definition

IDF measures importance of a word across all documents. 

Rare words get:

```text id="p9tx4t"
Higher IDF
```

Common words get:

```text id="0z9vgr"
Lower IDF
```

---

# 19. IDF Formula

IDF(t)=\log\left(\frac{\text{Total Documents}}{\text{Documents containing term }t}\right)

---

# 20. What is TF-IDF?

Most important concept.

## Definition

TF-IDF measures importance of a word in a document relative to entire corpus. 

Formula:

TF\text{-}IDF(t)=TF(t)\times IDF(t)

---

# 21. Why TF-IDF is Important?

## Viva Answer

TF-IDF helps identify important words while reducing impact of common words.

---

# 22. What is Feature Extraction in NLP?
